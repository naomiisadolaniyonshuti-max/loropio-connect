Why Loropio Connect

L.

Information can open doors.
Let’s make it easier to find.

Why we started

Useful information often travels by word of mouth or gets lost across different messages. This project is a simple, shared place to make community information easier to find and access.

Our hope

To help residents discover opportunities, support local businesses and feel more connected to what’s happening around them—one useful update at a time.

An individual community development project under Learning Lions.
